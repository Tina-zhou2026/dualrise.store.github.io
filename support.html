Frequently Asked Questions

What are binaural beats?
Binaural beats are an auditory illusion created when two slightly different frequencies are played in each ear. Your brain perceives the difference as a rhythmic beat. For example, if 200Hz plays in your left ear and 210Hz in your right, your brain hears a 10Hz beat. Research suggests this can influence brainwave patterns through a process called Frequency-Following Response (FFR).
Do I need headphones?
Yes, stereo headphones are required for the binaural effect. The left-right frequency difference is the mechanism that makes binaural beats work. Mono speakers or single-ear headphones won't create the effect. For noise and natural sounds, speakers work but the neural tone layer will be less effective.
Is AURA a medical device?
No. AURA is a wellness and lifestyle application, not a medical device. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease or medical condition, including insomnia, ADHD, anxiety, or tinnitus. If you have a medical condition, consult a healthcare professional before use.
Can I use AURA while driving?
No. Do not use AURA while driving or operating machinery. Binaural beats can cause drowsiness and disorientation, especially with Sleep and Calm presets. Safety first — only use AURA when you can safely relax or focus without needing to react to your environment.
How do I use AURA for ADHD focus?
For ADHD focus, we recommend: Deep Focus (15Hz Beta), ADHD Work Mode (Café + Beta combination), or White Noise (with embedded Beta). Start with 25-50 minute sessions. Use headphones for the binaural effect. The external rhythm provides "scaffolding" for attention. Combine with the café ambience if you work better with social energy around you.
Can AURA help with tinnitus?
AURA includes Blue and Violet noise colors specifically designed for high-frequency tinnitus masking. These emphasize the frequency range where many tinnitus sufferers experience phantom sounds (8-16kHz). While not a treatment, they may provide auditory masking comfort. Start at low volume and adjust to comfort.
Why is the app free?
AURA is currently in its free launch phase. All 36 presets are unlocked with no subscription. We may introduce premium features in the future, but core functionality will always remain accessible. No ads, no data selling, no tracking — your brainwaves are yours.
Does AURA collect my data?
No. AURA is built privacy-first. All session data, preferences, and usage patterns stay on your device. We do not track your listening habits, sell your data, or use third-party analytics SDKs. We don't even require an account. Deleting the app removes all data permanently.
How do I create custom combinations?
Go to the Combinations tab. Tap "Create New" and select up to 3 layers: a Neural Tone (binaural beats), a Noise Color, and/or an Environment (Natural or Human Space). Adjust each layer's volume independently. Save your combination with a custom name. You can create unlimited combinations.
The sound stops when I lock my screen. How do I fix this?
AURA supports background audio playback. If sound stops when you lock your screen, check: 1) iOS Settings > AURA > Background App Refresh is ON, 2) Control Center shows the audio player is active, 3) You haven't enabled Low Power Mode (which restricts background audio). If issues persist, restart the app or contact support.
Can I use AURA with Apple Watch?
Apple Watch integration is planned for a future update. You'll be able to start/stop sessions, adjust volume, and view basic controls from your wrist. HRV (heart rate variability) adaptive audio is also in development — the app will adjust frequencies based on your physiological state.

Quick Start Guide

For Sleep

  1. Choose Deep Sleep (1Hz Delta) or Abyss (2.5Hz Delta)
  2. Start 20-30 minutes before bed
  3. Volume: barely audible
  4. Use stereo headphones
  5. For severe insomnia, try Night Cocoon (3-layer combination)

For Focus

  1. Choose Deep Focus (15Hz Beta) or Flow State (40Hz Gamma)
  2. Set timer for 25-50 minutes (Pomodoro-style)
  3. Volume: moderate, not competing with thoughts
  4. Use stereo headphones for binaural effect
  5. For ADHD, try ADHD Work Mode combination

For Tinnitus Relief

  1. Choose Blue (30Hz High Beta) or Violet (20Hz Beta-Gamma)
  2. Start at low volume, adjust to comfort
  3. Use for 15-30 minute sessions
  4. Not recommended for sleep — brightness signals wakefulness
